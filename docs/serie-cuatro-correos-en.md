# Secret Key · four-email series (English)

**Status:** v3 draft, humanized with [guia-humanizar.md](guia-humanizar.md). Not scheduled or
sent. English version of [serie-cuatro-correos.md](serie-cuatro-correos.md), written to sound
like Tamara and not as a literal translation.

**Suggested rhythm:** day 0, day 3, day 7 and day 12. The series stops if the person replies,
asks to unsubscribe or the email bounces. Prices and terms are shared only with people who reply
with interest.

## Email 1

**Subject:** Twenty tabs open
_Variants:_ And none tells you which · Whose advice do you take?

**Preheader:** There is so much out there that you no longer know where to start.

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Think about the last time you looked for something that really mattered. A place, a person, a
practice that sat well with you.

Twenty tabs open. Six lists of "the best of." Your phone buzzing with something else. More and
more notifications and less and less presence, and then the sentence that slips out at night: I
can't take it anymore.

It happened to me. I spent hours and hours looking for recommendations. I was so overwhelmed that
I did not know where to start, and everything I found looked like what Google or each place's
website already said.

There was plenty of information. What I missed was someone I could trust who had already tried it.

Does that sound familiar? Reply with two words, it helps me to know.

Tamara Edo

## Email 2

**Subject:** Before it was called Secret Key
_Variants:_ I never set out to build anything · One door, then another

**Preheader:** KAP, constellations, the Enneagram, workshops and The Artist's Way.

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

I never sat down to build anything. I was looking.

I went through KAP sessions. Through constellations. Through the Enneagram, through workshops,
through _The Artist's Way_. Each one opened a different door, and all of them helped me.

What I did not know is that, without noticing, Secret Key was taking shape between one session and
the next.

After so much road, what I want most is to save time and detours for the person coming behind me.
To help them find, sooner, people who inspire them and feel like equals, and from there to know
themselves better and get along better with others and with their surroundings.

The name came much later.

Tamara Edo

## Email 3

**Subject:** What Secret Key is
_Variants:_ A circle of trust · Live local, wherever you are

**Preheader:** Someone in the circle tried it before you.

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Here it is in two lines. Secret Key is a circle of trust. Someone in the circle has already tried
the activities you will find inside, so when something is recommended to you, they lived it.

It happens in Chapters (the Mediterranean, Bali, Madrid and Miami), with an app and a calendar to
help us find each other. The rest happens between people. As equals, and your name is enough.

Our line is this: Live local, wherever you are.

To me it feels very old. Feeling at home with yourself, with the people near you and with the
place you are in. It is what is left when the noise goes quiet.

If a circle like that existed, what is the first thing you would want it to recommend?

Tamara Edo

## Email 4

**Subject:** Shall we talk for a while?
_Variants:_ If any of this rang true · Fifteen minutes, whenever suits you

**Preheader:** Reply with one sentence and we continue from there.

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

I have told you where Secret Key comes from: a lot of searching, a lot of doors and the wish that
other people find company on their own path.

Now I would like to know what resonated with you. Meeting the Circle, getting close to a Chapter,
trying an experience, finding a place to live local from. A fifteen minute conversation, whenever
suits you, is enough to start.

And if you have a space, a practice or an experience you would like to share with the Circle, we
can talk about that too. If that path interests you, I will explain calmly how it works: what is
shared with the Circle and the fees and commissions of each level. You will see everything before
you decide.

Reply with one of these lines:

- I want to meet the Circle
- I want to explore a collaboration
- Tell me what you are looking for, and I will tell you

I answer myself.

Tamara Edo
