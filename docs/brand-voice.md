# Secret Key — brand voice

Paste this wherever copy gets written outside this repo: GoHighLevel (AI prompt fields,
email and funnel templates, snippets), agency briefs, freelancers, decks. It stands on its
own.

---

## The rule that comes first

**Secret Key is always described by what it is.** Never by what it is not.

No "we are not a concierge". No "not a travel agency". No "what we are not" section.
In any page, email, SMS, funnel, ad, product description, deck or social post. Ever.

State what Secret Key **is**, and let the contrast happen in the reader's head.

## Positive language, always

Every negation-led sentence gets rewritten into the affirmative.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "We don't waste your time" | "We hand your time back" |
| "Not a luxury club" | "A membership measured in hours returned" |
| "Nobody remembers the errand" | "The evening is what stays" |
| "No hidden fees" | "One price, everything included" |
| "Don't miss out" | "The door is open this week" |
| "We do not sell your data" | "Your data is yours" |

Words that signal a rewrite is due: *not, never, nobody, no…, don't, can't, cannot,
refuse, without, avoid, stop, miss out.*

This holds in legal and policy copy too. Precision survives the rule: choose the
affirmative phrasing that says the same thing.

## What Secret Key is

A **time optimization and extraordinary access ecosystem**.

Every piece of copy reinforces that. The north star is minutes saved and minutes enjoyed —
time returned to members, and what those hours become.

## Tone

Calm. Confident. Understated. Short sentences. Warm, generous, human.

Luxury here sounds like restraint, never volume. Write the way you would speak to someone
you respect and have known for years: direct, unhurried, generous with the useful part.

Avoid: hype, urgency theatre, exclamation marks, buzzwords, hard-sell CTAs, apologising.

## Principles behind the words

1. Time is the ultimate luxury
2. Access beats ownership
3. Simplicity scales
4. Trust compounds
5. Experiences create memories
6. Community creates leverage

## Quick test before anything ships

1. Does it say what Secret Key **is**?
2. Is every sentence affirmative?
3. Does it reinforce time returned?
4. Would it sound right read aloud, calmly, to one person?

Four yeses, and it ships.
